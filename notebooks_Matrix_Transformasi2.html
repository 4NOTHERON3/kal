Transformasi Linear dan Translasi¶

1. Koordinat Titik¶

Koordinat Pada Gambar:

$$ A = (2,3), \quad B = (2,1), \quad C = (4,1) $$

$$ D = (2,4), \quad E = (2,0), \quad F = (4,0) $$

2. Bentuk Umum Transformasi¶

Misalkan transformasi linear:

$$ T(x,y) = (ax + by,\; cx + dy) $$

3. Menyusun Sistem Persamaan¶

Dari A → B¶

$$ T(2,3) = (2,1) $$

$$ 2a + 3b = 2 $$

$$ 2c + 3d = 1 $$

Dari D → E¶

$$ T(2,4) = (2,0) $$

$$ 2a + 4b = 2 $$

$$ 2c + 4d = 0 $$

4. Menyelesaikan Sistem¶

Untuk a dan b¶

$$ (2a + 4b) - (2a + 3b) = 2 - 2 $$

$$ b = 0 $$

$$ 2a = 2 \Rightarrow a = 1 $$

Untuk c dan d¶

$$ (2c + 4d) - (2c + 3d) = 0 - 1 $$

$$ d = -1 $$

$$ 2c + 3(-1) = 1 $$

$$ 2c - 3 = 1 \Rightarrow 2c = 4 \Rightarrow c = 2 $$

5. Matriks Transformasi¶

$$ T = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ 2 & -1 \end{bmatrix} $$

6. Fungsi Transformasi¶

$$ T(x,y) = (x,\; 2x - y) $$

7. Pengujian Transformasi¶

A → B¶

$$ T(2,3) = (2,\;4-3) = (2,1) $$

sesuai

B → C¶

$$ T(2,1) = (2,\;4-1) = (2,3) $$

$$ (2,3) \neq (4,1) $$

D → E¶

$$ T(2,4) = (2,\;4-4) = (2,0) $$

sesuai

E → F¶

$$ T(2,0) = (2,\;4-0) = (2,4) $$

$$ (2,4) \neq (4,0) $$

8. Transformasi Kedua (Translasi)¶

$$ B(2,1) \rightarrow C(4,1) $$

$$ E(2,0) \rightarrow F(4,0) $$

$$ T_2(x,y) = (x+2,\; y) $$

9. Ringkasan¶

Transformasi 1¶

$$ T_1(x,y) = (x,\; 2x - y) $$

$$ T_1 = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ 2 & -1 \end{bmatrix} $$

Transformasi 2¶

$$ T_2(x,y) = (x+2,\; y) $$

Transformasi dan Titik Bayangan¶

Diketahui Transformasi¶

Transformasi utama:

$$ T_1(x,y) = (x,\; 2x - y) $$

Translasi:

$$ T_2(x,y) = (x+2,\; y) $$

1. Hitung Titik Bayangan (menggunakan $T_1$)¶

A'¶

$$ A = (2,3) $$

$$ A' = T_1(2,3) = (2,\; 2(2) - 3) = (2,\; 4 - 3) = (2,1) $$

B'¶

$$ B = (2,1) $$

$$ B' = T_1(2,1) = (2,\; 2(2) - 1) = (2,\; 4 - 1) = (2,3) $$

C'¶

$$ C = (4,1) $$

$$ C' = T_1(4,1) = (4,\; 2(4) - 1) = (4,\; 8 - 1) = (4,7) $$

D'¶

$$ D = (2,4) $$

$$ D' = T_1(2,4) = (2,\; 2(2) - 4) = (2,\; 4 - 4) = (2,0) $$

E'¶

$$ E = (2,0) $$

$$ E' = T_1(2,0) = (2,\; 2(2) - 0) = (2,\; 4 - 0) = (2,4) $$

F'¶

$$ F = (4,0) $$

$$ F' = T_1(4,0) = (4,\; 2(4) - 0) = (4,\; 8 - 0) = (4,8) $$
